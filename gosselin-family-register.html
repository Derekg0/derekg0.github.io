    About this register

    Everything here is drawn from published obituaries and death notices — chiefly those of Robert Gosselin (2025) and Russell J. Gosselin (2006), plus the Lonczak family notices. Names of living relatives come from those same public records.

    The living generations come from those obituaries. The older generations — everything above the great‑grandparents — were traced in July 2026 through FamilySearch census, vital, and parish records: the French‑Canadian Gosselin line all the way to Gabriel Gosselin (1621, Combray, Normandy), the Irish line to the immigrant Jeremiah Brennan (b. 1861, County Kerry), and the Polish line to Michał Lonczak (b.~1846) & Agnieszka Puła of Piątkowiec in Zgórsko parish, Galicia.

    Two names listed in the 2006 obituary, Stephanie and Michael Gosselin (with Rusty's household but not blood children), are left off this register by request. On the old Katyń question: Ludwik's 1884 baptism names his parents as Michał Lonczak & Agnieszka Puła of Piątkowiec — the very details once thought to belong to a different Ludwik. Ours emigrated in 1904 and died in Chicopee in 1938, so he was never a Katyń victim; the earlier note simply had the identification backwards.
